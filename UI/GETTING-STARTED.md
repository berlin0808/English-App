# Quick Vocab Trainer - Getting Started

## ✨ What is this?

Quick Vocab Trainer is a simple vocabulary learning app that runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no signup, no accounts needed. Everything stays on your computer.

## 🚀 Quick Start (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Open the App
- Double-click `index.html` in your browser, OR
- Right-click → Open with → Your browser

### Step 2: Add Your First Word
1. Click the **📝 Quick Add** tab
2. Type a word (e.g., "sophisticated")
3. Add its meaning (e.g., "advanced and refined")
4. Click **💾 Save Word**
5. Congratulations! Your first word is saved! 🎉

### Step 3: Take a Quiz
1. Click the **🧠 Quiz** tab
2. Click **🚀 Start Quiz**
3. Select the correct answer
4. Click **Submit Answer**
5. Learn from the feedback!

## 📚 Complete Feature Guide

### Tab 1: 📝 Quick Add (Vocabulary Entry)
**Purpose**: Add new words you encounter while reading

**How to use**:
1. **Word/Phrase**: Enter the word (required)
2. **Meaning**: Enter what it means (required)
3. **Example Sentence**: Add an example if you want (optional)
4. **Source**: Remember where you found it (optional)

**Tips**:
- Use "Save & Add Another" to add multiple words quickly
- Add examples to remember context better
- Note the source so you can review materials later

---

### Tab 2: 📋 Word List (Your Vocabulary)
**Purpose**: Browse and manage all your words

**Features**:
- **Search**: Type to find words instantly
- **View All**: See your complete vocabulary
- **Word Info**: Shows meaning, example, source, and mastery level
- **Delete**: Remove words you don't need

**Mastery Levels**:
- 🔴 Unknown (0) - Haven't studied yet
- 🟡 Recognized (1) - Can recognize it
- 🟢 Understood (2) - Can understand it
- 💙 Can Use (3) - Can use it in writing
- 💚 Mastered (4) - Complete mastery!

---

### Tab 3: 🧠 Quiz (Practice & Test)
**Purpose**: Test yourself and reinforce learning

**Quiz Types**:
1. **Word → Meaning**: See a word, pick its meaning
2. **Meaning → Word**: See a meaning, pick the word

**How to take a quiz**:
1. Choose quiz type
2. Click "Start Quiz"
3. Read the question
4. Click an option to select it
5. Click "Submit Answer"
6. Get immediate feedback
7. Click "Next Question" to continue

**Feedback Explained**:
- **✓ Correct!**: Well done! Moving to next question
- **✗ Incorrect**: Shows the right answer for learning

**After Quiz**:
- See your score (e.g., 8/10)
- Get encouragement message
- Option to take another quiz
- Can review words you missed

---

### Tab 4: ✍️ Write (Sentence Practice)
**Purpose**: Practice using words in real sentences

**How to use**:
1. Click "✍️ Start Writing Practice"
2. Read the target word and its meaning
3. Write a sentence using that word
4. Click "✨ Check Sentence"
5. Review feedback on grammar and naturalness
6. Click "Next Word" to continue

**Feedback Includes**:
- Grammar check ✓
- Naturalness score (1-5)
- Alternative phrasing suggestion
- Example from your vocabulary

**Tips**:
- Write natural sentences, not just examples
- Try to use the word in context
- Check multiple times if unsure

---

### Tab 5: 📊 Progress (Learning Stats)
**Purpose**: Track your learning journey

**Metrics Shown**:
- **Total Words**: How many words you have
- **Mastered**: Fully learned
- **Learning**: Still studying
- **Quiz Accuracy**: Your overall quiz success rate

**Streaks**:
- **Current**: Days studied in a row
- **Longest**: Best streak ever

**Activity Chart**:
- Weekly visual chart of your studying
- See which days you studied most

**Mastery Distribution**:
- Pie-like view of where you are with all words
- Quick overview of learning progress

---

### Tab 6: ⚙️ Settings (Customization)
**Purpose**: Customize app to your needs

**Study Preferences**:
- **Quiz Size**: How many questions per quiz (5/10/20/50)
- **AI Feedback**: Enable/disable sentence checking
- **API Key**: Add OpenAI key for better AI feedback

**Appearance**:
- **Theme**: Light/Dark/Auto (matches your system)
- **Font Size**: Small/Normal/Large

**Data Management**:
- **Export Data**: Save as JSON backup
- **Import Data**: Load from backup
- **Reset Progress**: Clear quiz results but keep words
- **Delete All**: Complete data wipe (use with caution!)

---

## 💾 Data & Backup

### Where is my data saved?
- Automatically saved in your browser's storage
- No internet needed
- All data stays on your computer

### How to backup?
1. Go to **⚙️ Settings**
2. Click **📥 Export Data (JSON)**
3. A file downloads automatically
4. Keep this file safe!

### How to restore from backup?
1. Go to **⚙️ Settings**
2. Click **📤 Import Data**
3. Select your backup file
4. Your data is restored!

## 🎯 Best Learning Practices

### Daily Routine
1. **Spend 5 min**: Add new words while reading
2. **Spend 10 min**: Take a quick 10-word quiz
3. **Spend 10 min**: Practice writing 3-5 sentences
4. **Spend 5 min**: Review your progress

**Total: 30 minutes per day for great results!**

### Tips for Success
1. ✅ **Add context**: Always include example sentences
2. ✅ **Study consistently**: A few minutes daily beats cramming
3. ✅ **Use all features**: Quiz + writing practice = faster learning
4. ✅ **Review regularly**: See your progress grow in the Progress tab
5. ✅ **Practice writing**: Writing helps internalize words faster

### When to Reset
- After completing one topic/book
- When you want a fresh start
- Every semester/month to track progress

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Do I need an account?
**A:** No! No account, no login, no email needed.

### Q: Is my data secure?
**A:** Yes! Everything is stored locally on your computer only.

### Q: Can I use this offline?
**A:** Yes! The app works 100% offline.

### Q: How many words can I add?
**A:** As many as you want! Limited only by your browser's storage.

### Q: What if I accidentally delete a word?
**A:** Use your backup! Export regularly to backup.

### Q: Can I use this on mobile?
**A:** It works in mobile browsers, though desktop is recommended.

### Q: Is there a word limit?
**A:** Most modern browsers support 5-10 MB of storage, which is thousands of words!

## ⚠️ Important Notes

1. **Browser matters**: Works best in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari
2. **Clear cache**: Don't clear browser data if you want to keep your words!
3. **Multiple devices**: Data doesn't sync between devices (export/import to move it)
4. **Backup**: Regularly export your data as backup
